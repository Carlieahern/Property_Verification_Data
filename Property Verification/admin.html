Which columns can I include?

Recognised as-is: Property Name, RM Name, Property Code, Revenue Management, Does the property use HappyCo?, Phone Landline Number, Do residents call maintenance directly for emergencies?, If yes what is the number, Answering Service Provider, Directions to Forward, Directions to remove the forwarding, Office Hours, Completed by, CM/SM Name.

Optional extras: Transition Date (per property), and RM Email so reminders can be sent.

Office hours can come in either shape, and both load straight into the day/time picker for the Regional to verify:

• one Office Hours cell such as Mon-Fri 9am-6pm, Sat 10-4, Sun Closed, or
• per-day columns: Monday Hours … Sunday Hours (values like 9:00 AM-6:00 PM or Closed), or Monday Open + Monday Close pairs.
Any day you do not mention is recorded as Closed, and the Regional still has to confirm it on screen.
